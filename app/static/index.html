Retrieves once, then generates a grounded answer.

Answers are generated by a language model running on our server, grounded only in the document text shown in each source excerpt — they may still be incomplete or wrong; verify anything important against the excerpt itself.

Known limitations: the system can still be misled by text embedded inside a document that tries to override its real content (tested and substantially reduced, not eliminated — see the project writeup for measured rates). Document ambiguity (a question matching more than one upload) is detected and asks for clarification in common cases, not all. This is a portfolio demo, not a product — do not upload real personal or financial documents.
